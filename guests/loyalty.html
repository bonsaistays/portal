Common Questions

Good to know.

How do I join Bon Voyage?

Membership is invite-only — you'll receive an invite code with any Bonsai Stays booking confirmation, or an existing member can invite you directly through the app. This keeps the community quality high.

Do points expire?

Points are valid for 24 months from the date of your last qualifying stay. As long as you keep staying, your points keep building.

Can I share my membership with family?

Each account is individual, but you can invite family members to join as their own members — and if they're staying with you at a booking, you can share property access directly through the app.

How do I redeem my points?

Points can be redeemed for credit toward future stays, experience add-ons, or partner perks — all from within the app. Our team will also reach out when you hit key milestones.

Is there a fee to join?

Bon Voyage membership is completely free. You just need an invite code and a Bonsai Stays booking to get started.

Ready to join?

Start your journey.

Book your first stay and your invite code will arrive with your confirmation. Or reach out and we'll point you in the right direction.
