Our Collections

Five ways to stay with us

Every Bonsai property is professionally managed to our standard. Collections are how we group them — by character, by comfort level, and by the kind of experience you're after.

"Where luxury becomes your norm."

Reserve properties are our flagship luxury tier — thoughtfully designed spaces with premium finishes, designer interiors, and curated amenities that make every stay feel like a special occasion. Expect hotel-grade linens, high-end appliances, and that unmistakable feeling of arriving somewhere exceptional.

Designer interiors Premium linens High-end appliances 3+ bedrooms Concierge support

Bonsai Green
"Luxurious living, lighter footprint."

Green properties are built and operated with the environment in mind. Sustainable materials, energy-conscious design, and deep immersion in the natural landscape. You don't sacrifice comfort — you gain a different kind of richness. These are spaces that make you feel connected to something bigger.

Eco-certified Nature-immersed Sustainable materials Low-impact design Local sourcing

Unique Stays
Bonsai Originals
"Stays worth writing home about."

Originals are the properties that stop you mid-scroll. Converted heritage barns, bold architectural statements, properties with stories. Each one is genuinely one-of-a-kind — you won't find anything quite like it anywhere else. If you're booking for the experience as much as the destination, this is your collection.

Unique architecture Heritage character Memorable design Instagrammable One-of-a-kind
