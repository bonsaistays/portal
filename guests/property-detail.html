About this property

Nestled among the rolling vineyards of Prince Edward County, The Vineyard House is a beautifully restored heritage home that blends rustic charm with modern comforts. Wake up to panoramic views of the vines, enjoy morning coffee on the wraparound porch, and spend your afternoons exploring the County's world-class wineries and sandy beaches just minutes away.

Professionally managed by Bonsai Stays, every turnover is handled to hotel standards — fresh premium linens, fully stocked amenities, and a detailed welcome guide to the best spots in the County.
