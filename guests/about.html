Our Story

We raised the standard.

Bonsai Stays was born out of frustration. Too many short-term rentals were inconsistent — beautiful photos online, mediocre reality on arrival. Scratchy linens, half-empty amenities, unresponsive hosts.

We set out to build something different: a platform where every property is managed to the same exacting standard, and where guests can layer on curated experiences that turn a good trip into an unforgettable one.

Today, Bonsai Stays is Ontario's most trusted short-term rental brand — for guests who won't compromise, and for owners who want their properties to perform.

Our Mission

We believe that the best travel experiences don't happen at hotels — they happen in the places that feel like a home, just better than your own. Professionally managed, beautifully stocked, and thoughtfully enhanced with experiences that match the setting.

Our mission is to make that level of quality the default, not the exception — across every cottage, chalet, and city suite in our collection.

What We Stand For

The Bonsai standard.

Every decision we make — from which properties we onboard to how we fold a duvet — comes back to these principles.

🌿

Uncompromising Quality

We visit every property before it joins our platform. If it doesn't meet the Bonsai standard, it doesn't get listed. Period. Once onboarded, our teams inspect after every turnover.

🤝

Genuine Hospitality

Warm, responsive, human. Our guest support team is real people who genuinely care about your stay. We don't hide behind auto-replies when something matters.

📍

Local Roots

Every region we operate in has a dedicated local team. We're not managing remotely — we know the roads, the suppliers, and the best breakfast spots in every market.

♻️

Responsible Travel

We partner with local businesses, source amenities regionally, and offer eco-friendly linen and cleaning options. Travel should leave places better than we found them.

🔍

Radical Transparency

No hidden fees, no misleading photos. What you see is what you get — and then some. If there's anything to know about a property, it's in the listing.

✨

Continuous Improvement

We read every review, measure every metric, and act on every piece of feedback. The standard we hold today is higher than the one we held last year.
