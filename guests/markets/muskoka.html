Area Guide

The best of Muskoka.

Beyond your cottage door — towns, trails, and treasures worth exploring.

🏘️

Huntsville

Algonquin Theatre, farmers markets, and the gateway to Algonquin Park. Charming downtown worth an afternoon.

🛶

Port Carling

The hub of the Muskoka Lakes. Boutique shops, restaurant patios on the water, and the iconic locks connecting three lakes.

🌲

Algonquin Park

Canada's oldest provincial park, 90 minutes east. Canoe routes, wildlife, and fall colours that belong on a postcard.

🍷

Gravenhurst

Opera house, wharf district, and the Muskoka Brewery. The southernmost town in cottage country with a strong local dining scene.
