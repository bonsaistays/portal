Year-Round Destination

Every season has its reason.

Collingwood and Wasaga Beach deliver completely different experiences depending on when you visit — and we're here to make each one exceptional.

❄️ Winter

Ski Season

Blue Mountain and Osler Bluff are minutes away. Arrive to a warm chalet with everything you need for a perfect ski weekend.

☀️ Summer

Beach & Georgian Bay

Wasaga Beach is the world's longest freshwater beach. Summer days on the sand, evenings on the deck — perfect.

🍂 Fall

Trail & Colour Season

The Niagara Escarpment turns gold and crimson. Hiking, cycling, wineries, and the most dramatic sunsets of the year.

🌸 Spring

Quiet Season Escapes

Fewer crowds, lower rates, and the landscape coming alive. Mountain bike trails opening, Georgian Bay flat and glassy.
