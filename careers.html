Why Bonsai Stays

Bonsai Stays is Ontario's premium short-term rental brand — managing a curated collection of properties across cottage country, Collingwood, Toronto and beyond. We don't just list homes; we manage every detail of the guest experience from first click to check-out.

Behind that experience is a team that cares — about quality, about people, and about building something they're genuinely proud to work on.

Who Thrives Here

What we look for.

We don't have a rigid checklist. We hire people who embody these qualities — then trust them to grow.

01

High Ownership

You treat every task like it has your name on it. No detail is someone else's problem, and you follow through without being reminded.

02

Genuine Warmth

You understand that hospitality is a feeling, not a checklist. Every interaction — guest, owner or colleague — gets your full attention and care.

03

Curiosity

You ask why things work the way they do, look for better ways of doing them, and aren't afraid to suggest ideas — even when they push back on the status quo.

04

Calm Under Pressure

Hospitality moves fast. When things go sideways, you stay even-keeled, problem-solve calmly, and keep the guest experience at the centre.

05

Attention to Detail

You notice things others miss — the wrong pillow arrangement, a typo in a listing, the one line in a guest message that changes the entire tone. That eye matters here.

06

Team First

We're building this together. You share knowledge freely, celebrate the team's wins as much as your own, and make the people around you better at what they do.
