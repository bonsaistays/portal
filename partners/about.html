Our Story

Built by operators, for operators.

We heard the frustration firsthand. Hosts and property managers across Ontario were struggling to find reliable housekeeping and service companies they could actually count on — teams that showed up, met the standard, and treated every property like it was their own.

With deep experience in the hospitality and hotel industry, we knew the standard that guests expected. We also knew the industry wasn't delivering it consistently for short-term rentals. So we decided to make a change.

Bonsai Stays was built to bridge that gap — bringing hotel-grade operations, reliable local teams, and genuine hospitality to the short-term rental market across Ontario.

Today, we're trusted by 80+ property owners and managers across three regions — and growing.

How We Got Here

Built from the ground up.

Every milestone has made us better. Here's how Bonsai Stays grew from a single property operation into Ontario's most trusted hospitality partner.

S'24
September 2024

Entering the Muskoka market

After hearing the frustrations of two property management companies with their existing providers, we entered the Muskoka market with our linen rental program — determined to raise the bar.

M'25
May 2025

Housekeeping joins the lineup

After growing our linen rental services from 3 properties to over 15, we evolved to include housekeeping — driven entirely by demand we saw from our existing partners.

A'25
August 2025

The full changeover package launches

With 15+ housekeeping properties and 25 linen rental properties, we launched the full changeover package — making turnovers more seamless and straightforward for owners and property managers.

M'26
May 2026

80+ properties & expanding markets

Now with 80+ properties across our linen and housekeeping services, we work with the top property management companies in Muskoka and have grown into the GTA — with Collingwood and Wasaga Beach coming soon.

→
2026 Onwards

The road ahead

We look forward to continuing our growth — expanding into new markets and building the premier platform to discover curated rentals across Ontario and beyond, complete with bookable in-home experiences during your stay.
