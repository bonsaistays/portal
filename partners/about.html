Our Story

Built by operators, for operators.

Bonsai Stays wasn't founded by software entrepreneurs or real estate investors. It was built by people who had actually managed short-term rental properties — and grown frustrated with the gap between what guests expected and what the industry delivered.

We set out to create the operations partner we wished had existed when we were owners: expert local teams, transparent reporting, hotel-grade standards, and a genuine commitment to making every property perform at its best.

Today, we're trusted by 200+ property owners and managers across six regions in Ontario.

How We Got Here

Built from the ground up.

Every milestone has made us better. Here's how Bonsai Stays grew from a single property operation into Ontario's most trusted hospitality partner.

20
2020

The idea takes root

Founded with 3 properties in Muskoka after seeing firsthand how inconsistent self-management was impacting guest reviews and owner revenue.

21
2021

Linen program launches

Developed our signature linen program after realising it was the single biggest driver of 5-star cleanliness reviews. 25 properties onboarded by year end.

22
2022

Expanding across Ontario

Opened regional teams in Blue Mountain, Prince Edward County, and Niagara. Reached 75 managed properties and launched our guest experience division.

23
2023

Guest platform & in-stay experiences

Launched the Bonsai Stays guest-facing platform, connecting travellers with our managed properties and introducing bookable in-stay experiences.

26
2026

200+ properties & growing

Operating across all six Ontario regions with a full suite of six services. Trusted by independent owners and multi-property portfolio managers alike.

What Drives Us

Our operating principles.

The values that shape every decision — from how we onboard a property to how we respond to a guest complaint at 2am.

🔬

Standards over shortcuts

We follow our 47-point checklist every time, even when it's faster not to. The standard exists because corners get cut, and guests notice.

📞

Owners treated like partners

You'll always have a direct line to your account manager. No ticketing systems, no form submissions. Real people, real conversations.

📍

Local knowledge is irreplaceable

Our teams live in the regions they serve. That means faster response times, better supplier relationships, and genuine local insight.

📊

Data-informed, not data-driven

We track everything, but we never let a metric override common sense. Numbers inform our decisions — they don't make them.

🤝

No lock-in, ever

Month-to-month agreements only. We believe the best retention strategy is to be so good you'd never want to leave — not to trap you in a contract.

🌱

Long-term thinking

We'd rather lose a client in the short term than overpromise and underdeliver. Our 98% retention rate reflects that approach.
